Archus.

The keeper of the backlog. One home per ticket, one qualified ID, no central duplicate to drift out of sync.

Archus owns your work-to-do: he creates, edits, and closes tickets where they belong, aggregates across your repos, and enforces one ticket, one home. Public writes are intent that routes to his brain; the GitHub bookkeeping stays private.
