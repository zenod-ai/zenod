## Method

- **Code:** production as live (`cloud.zenod.dev/api/health` sha `dce775e`), built from `main`.
- **Vault:** a read-only copy of Jordi's vault at revision `7f35d26` (2026-11-08 15:52Z), in a private folder on VPS3. Each run works on a throwaway copy and never pushes.
- **His real questions:** the two questions he asked the cloud Chat tab on 2026-11-08 (from his screenshots and relaunch feedback), four `ask_brain` calls on his live account through the team's Zenod connection today, and his WhatsApp messages of the last two weeks.
- **Test set:** 45 questions in his own words or in the shape he uses. 20 were written here from his voice notes of 25 Oct to 8 Nov. 25 came from the parallel accuracy worker (E12-S1b round 3, `run_6ad10161`), merged into the same file. Each question has an expected answer grounded in his notes, the Log references it comes from, and word groups for a mechanical score. The set lives privately on VPS3 next to the vault, never in the repo.
- **Runner:** [`e15-q1-eval/run.mjs`](e15-q1-eval/run.mjs). `chat` runs the cloud chat's own answer path (`streamAnswerFromMemory`). `ask` runs the MCP `ask_brain` path (`engine.ask`, 180 s cap). `--retrieval-only` runs ranking alone with no model and no cost. `score` scores answers captured elsewhere. Two scores per question: *facts* (the answer carries the expected word groups) and *memory* (one of the five memories shown is the source note or a page that cites it). The 20 answers written here were also judged by hand: makes sense, partly, or no.
- **Demo version:** [`e15-q1-eval/demo-questions.jsonl`](e15-q1-eval/demo-questions.jsonl) holds 20 questions over the made-up demo vault (`e12-s1b-bench/gen_vault.py`), so anyone can run it without private data:
  `python3 docs/evidence/e12-s1b-bench/gen_vault.py packages/core/test/fixtures/tree-vault /tmp/demo-vault`, then
  `node docs/evidence/e15-q1-eval/run.mjs chat . /tmp/demo-vault docs/evidence/e15-q1-eval/demo-questions.jsonl /tmp/out.json --retrieval-only` (or pipe an OpenRouter key on stdin without the flag).

## Causes, ranked (the 20 judged by hand, plus the live calls)

1. **Search matches words only:** 7 of 20. Paraphrases miss. His Spanish notes never match English questions. Older notes sharing a word beat the right newer note.
2. **The answer check discards right answers:** 4 of 20. The right note was in the top 5, once at rank 1, and the answer was hidden as "could not check".
3. **Agent reports outnumber his notes:** 313 of the 343 Log entries of 1 to 8 Nov are agent reports (201 ops roll-ups), 30 are his. Two answers were built from agent reports.
4. **No time-span questions:** "top five topics last week" and "what did I send on <date>" fail in both sets. The chat reads 8 ranked memories and does not filter by date.
5. **`ask_brain` is slow and blocked during filing:** 0 of 4 live answers. On the same code it took 10 to 157 s or failed its protocol, and puts internal labels in the text.
6. **Filing fragments a note:** one 8 Nov voice note became 9 new small pages, spreading its topic thin (close to E12-S8's open landing work).
