## Findings beyond the price

- **Answers 1 and 4 were "Memory filing is in progress. Please retry your question shortly." and were billed** ($0.035 and $0.034). Single, serial questions on a build holding the T7-F3 fix `c5164904`. T7-F3's row said to fix the remaining cases "only if T7-F5 or a tester shows one": this shows one. Handed to the Delivery Manager by desk note.
- **Answer 3: `POST /api/chat` took 125 s** (the browser got the edge's HTML timeout page). The server returned 200 at 02:49:41Z; its usage line and its cap charge came at 02:51:01Z. It read 97K input tokens for a one-line question.
- Answer 2 said "I couldn't verify an answer from source text read for this question", although the birds were saved on 12 Nov.
- Haiku 5.5 made no call in the window, so its pricing was checked against the list only.
- Spend: $0.206 real, just over the $0.20 limit set in the brief, because answer 3 read 97K tokens.

## Fix

[#1652](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1652) `packages/server/src/usageStore.ts`: a cache-read share per model (Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 0.05, default 0.1), Opus 5.5 at $4/$20 (was the $5/$25 Opus family rate), Haiku 5.5 long-prompt rate over 100K tokens. `creditRoute.test.ts` pins the four production answers at their real price and the 12 Nov answer at $0.070645. 121 tests in the 8 cost-related files and 96 in the usage, allowance and credit files pass. The wrong count only stopped testers early (the safe direction), so no rollback. Production moves with the next promote; then one answer is checked again.
