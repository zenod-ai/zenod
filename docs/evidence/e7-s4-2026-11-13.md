## Fix

[#1652](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1652) `packages/server/src/usageStore.ts`: a cache-read share per model (Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 0.05, default 0.1), Opus 5.5 at $4/$20 (was the $5/$25 Opus family rate), Haiku 5.5 long-prompt rate over 100K tokens. `creditRoute.test.ts` pins the four production answers at their real price and the 12 Nov answer at $0.070645. 121 tests in the 8 cost-related files and 96 in the usage, allowance and credit files pass. The wrong count only stopped testers early (the safe direction), so no rollback. Production moves with the next promote; then one answer is checked again.
