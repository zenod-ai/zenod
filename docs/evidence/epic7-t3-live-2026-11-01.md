## Capped-reply proof on prod (no real user)

The cap is one global figure, and there is no per-tenant override. Each tenant's month-to-date spend is compared against it. Making a test tenant over the cap therefore needs a test tenant whose own ledger is at $3. The steps followed the "M2 Bxx synthetic" rehearsal precedent (51 such tenants created and then `deleted` via the control plane):

1. 22:10:24 `POST /api/tenants` (control plane) created `e7-t3-capproof-20261101`, "EPIC-7 T3 cap proof synthetic". It has no account, no Stripe, no Phylax binding and no phone.
2. MCP `initialize` and `tools/list` with its token at `https://cloud.zenod.dev/mcp`. The ledger was empty.
3. One synthetic row was seeded into **that tenant's own** `usage.sqlite` (`operation=e7-t3-cap-proof-seed`, provider `synthetic`, $3, succeeded). The test then waited 65 s, past the 60 s spend cache.
4. 22:11:31 `chat_with_zenod` with `surface: whatsapp` and an `idempotencyKey`, the durable job path Phylax uses for WhatsApp text. Then a poll with `get_task_result`.
5. Result at 22:11:34: job `e3b0975b…` `kind: chat`, `state: done`, `isError: false` (so Phylax passes it through word for word), reply text:
   > ⚠️ You have used this month's AI allowance on your Zenod plan, so I can't process this message. It was not saved. Your allowance resets on 1 December 2026; please send it again then.
6. **Zero provider calls:** after the chat, the tenant's ledger still held only the seed row. Every model attempt is recorded there, failed ones included, and there were none. The container log shows `[task-job] e3b0975b-7915-4270-8d83-892c1805b282 stopped at the AI allowance: chat` and no LLM lines for the tenant.
7. Cleanup at 22:11:34: the seed row was deleted (1 row), and `DELETE /api/tenants/e7-t3-capproof-20261101` returned 200 `deleted`. The proof script was removed from VPS1. After the cleanup: health ok, readiness ready=true, Phylax bindings as above, tester-watch OK.

Not covered here, by design: the WhatsApp leg to a real phone at the cap. T7 still covers that ("a test account pushed to its cap is stopped with the message"). Media at the cap (archived raw, `ai_allowance_exhausted` receipt) is covered by the #1446 tests, not by a live run.
