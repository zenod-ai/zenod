# E12-S3d evidence: "Epic Spine" finds "EpicSpine" (2026-11-08)

Row: S3d in [EPIC-12](../zenod-app-chat-first-memory-graph-retrie.md). Follow-up to S3c ([evidence](e12-s3c-2026-11-08.md)), whose known limit was that a question written "Epic Spine" (two words) did not match the page "EpicSpine" (17 %).

## Cause

The index tokenizer keeps "EpicSpine" as one token, so the words "epic" and "spine" matched it only through the prefix `epic*`; "spine" matched nothing, and coverage credited half the question. The reverse also failed: "AgentTeam" in a question never matched a page titled "Agent Team".

## Fix (`packages/core/src/vault/memoryIndex.ts`, `buildMatchTerms`)

- Adjacent content words of the question are also searched joined: "Epic Spine" adds `"epicspine"`. It is an OR alternative of both words, so coverage credits the joined match as covering both.
- A camel-case word in the question is also searched split as a phrase: "EpicSpine" adds `"epic spine"` (matches "Epic Spine" and "epic-spine", since punctuation is a separator), "DevOps" adds `"dev ops"`.
- No schema change, no reindex: only the query side changed. Titles and aliases are searched in their stored spelling.
