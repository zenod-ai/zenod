## The rules (packages/server/src/calmCards.ts)

| Rule | What happens |
|---|---|
| One calm card a day | The seven helpers under the five personas (Remind me: Open loops, Weekly recap, People and follow-ups; Your ideas: Ideas digest; New possibilities: Something to try; Reflect: Patterns and ideas; Make it easier: Easier ways) put at most **one** card a day (UTC) in a user's inbox, together. |
| The others | **Wait**, they are not dropped: a scheduled helper that wakes after today's card has landed does not read or spend, its period stays open, it wakes again in the next night's window. It waits once a day (run log key `waits:<day>`). |
| Who goes first | The calm helper whose last card is oldest (never posted: first), so a nightly helper cannot crowd out a weekly one. |
| Reflect | Keeps its own once-in-3-days gap on top. |
| Run now | The user's own press is not held back; its card counts as the day's card. |
| One card a run | A calm helper posts at most one card in a run. |
| Keep my memory tidy | Clarifier, Topic gardener, Memory health are **outside** the five and **do not count** towards the day's card (they ask about the memory itself, have their own limits and are off for testers). The no-repeat rule applies to them too. |
| Never twice | Every card a helper posts leaves small keys in the user's persona store (`persona_shown`, newest 1000 kept): its words, its change, its pair of cited memories (two or more; the helper's own notes never count). A later card with any key already shown, or matching a helper card still in the inbox (cards from before S50 count), is left out by every helper; the run ends done and says "Left out a card already shown once". |
| Dismissed | A card put away with Not now never comes back. Bring it back on that same card is the only way back (it reopens the card; nothing new is posted). |
