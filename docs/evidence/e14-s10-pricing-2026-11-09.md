# Price and limits for personas, calls and desk jobs: proposal

Date: 2026-11-09 · Run: run_cb69a26b-5881-45f5-a80f-5ba5c3facaf2 · Epic: EPIC-14 (personas, inbox, voice and calls) · Proposal only: no price, Stripe product or plan was changed.

**Bottom line.** We recommend keeping the €9 Hosted plan as it is and including personas, calls and desk jobs in it, inside the $3 monthly AI allowance and with the caps now in the code. A typical user costs us about **$1.30 to $2.40** of AI a month with all three switched on, which is 13 to 23% of the €9 price. A heavy user like you costs at most **$6** a month, because answers, personas, calls and desk jobs all stop at the $3 allowance and only filing carries on, to $6. The €9 plan covers both. We do not recommend an add-on or a higher plan before the first five testers have used Zenod for a month, because we have no real usage of calls or personas to price them on.

"Typical" is a few voice notes and questions a day on a small memory, personas on, two short calls and ten desk jobs a month. "Heavy" is your use today: about four notes a day into a large memory, plus everything else switched on and used.

## 2. Does €9 cover it?

€9 is about **$10.40**. Card fees take about **$0.45** (1.5% plus €0.25 on a European card), leaving about **$9.95**.

| Who | AI cost a month | Left from €9 after card fees and AI | AI share of the price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical user, everything on | $1.30 to $2.40 | **$7.55 to $8.65** | 13 to 23% |
| Heavy user, everything on | $6.00 at most (about $5.50 in practice) | **about $4** | up to 58% |
| Heavy user if nothing were capped | about $7.50 | about $2.50 | 72% |

Hosting and support per user are not in these numbers: Zenod runs on one shared server today and its cost is not split per user. Yes, €9 covers both a typical and a heavy user with the caps in place. The caps are what keep a heavy user above water: without them a heavy user with long calls would cost more than the plan.

What a heavy user notices: answers stop when the $3 is used (for you, around the 19th of the month), personas, calls and desk jobs pause a little before, at $2.50. Notes are always kept and filed. The Usage tab shows each of these, with what it costs.

## 3. Options

| Option | What the customer pays | What changes | Work for us | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Include in €9 with these caps (recommended)** | €9, as now | nothing for customers. Personas, calls and desk jobs share the $3 allowance with their own caps | none: it is live in the code, the caps change in one setting | a heavy user runs out of answers before the month ends. Worst case is $6 of AI per user |
| B. Add-on "Calls and personas", €4 a month | €13 with the add-on | calls up to $5 a month (about 3 hours), personas up to $3, its own $8 allowance | a new Stripe price, an entitlement on the account, the caps read per account: about 2 days | an add-on nobody asked for yet; priced on two test calls |
| C. Higher plan "Zenod Plus", €19 a month | €19 | $8 allowance, calls $4, personas $2, desk $1. Fits a heavy user all month | a second Stripe plan, the plan picker, per-plan caps: about 2 to 3 days | splits a tiny tester base across two plans before we know who is heavy |

**Recommendation: option A now.** It needs no price change, no Stripe work and nothing from testers, who are on the free pass anyway. It keeps the worst case at $6 of AI on a €9 plan. Look again after the first five testers have had a month (the go and no-go step of the tester epic): if real users hit the call or persona caps, offer option B; if several heavy users run out of answers, offer option C. The numbers to decide on are in the new admin view, spend per feature per tenant, from this month on.

## What is decided where

- The limits model and its caps are code: one place, changeable with one setting (`ZENOD_LIMITS_JSON`) and a restart, without new code.
- What customers pay is your call. This page is the proposal; the Delivery Manager raises it as an ask.
