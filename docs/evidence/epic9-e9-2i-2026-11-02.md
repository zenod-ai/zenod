## 2. Fixes (code: `packages/core/src/engine/…`)

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| A subject the person keeps naming has no page, so clustering can't find it | New `treeSubjects.ts`, **named subjects**. It collects the vault's coined names: identifiers written as one word (`agent_team`, `EpicSpine`, `Poly-Maker`), excluding stopwords, filler, ingest words and ids. It counts them in the person's own captures of the last 30 days in any spelling ("agent team", "Agent-Team"), with ingest headers removed. A name said ≥ 10 times, in ≥ 3 captures, on ≥ 2 days, with no tree page titled after it, gets `Projects/<Name>.md` (`generated: zenod-subject`). The page has one cited line per capture. Agent reports never count. Later runs append new own captures and never edit anyone else's page. The weekly sweep and `zenod tree discover --apply` both run it. |
| A subject page has to stand as its own topic | `treeDiscover.ts`: a subject page is a topic of its own, together with the pages filed under it. Subject topics never merge with another topic. A cold subject topic is a long-term area of its own, whatever its size. |
| A page the person captures about on its own gets merged into a neighbour | `treeDiscover.ts`, **subjects of their own**. A page splits off its topic, with the pages under it, when all of these hold: it holds ≥ 5 own captures; ≥ 85% of them sit on no other page of the topic; its title shares no word with the topic's other titles; and it is not what most of the topic hangs under. On Jordi's vault only the family page meets this. |
| The root is named after one page's long title | When the person says the head of the hub title in their own captures but never says its tail, the head names the root: "Family". Titles the person says in full, or whose head they never say, are unchanged ("Browser Automation and Jev", "Alpha9 Tax and AEAT"). |
