The fix

Each Claude model now has its own cached-input price: Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 at 5% of input, the rest at 10%. Opus 5.5 also gets its own $4 and $20 price, and Haiku 5.5 gets its higher price for prompts over 100,000 tokens. A test pins these four production answers at their real prices. Change 1652. It reaches production with the next promote, then one more answer is checked.
