6   Where today sits Live

Today's €9 plan is Tier 1, unchanged. You would be on Tier 2.

Tier 1 has the same $3 allowance and the same limits that run now, so nobody who pays €9 loses anything.

  • Today's €9 plan. $3 allowance, filing to $6, personas $1, desk $0.50 and 20 a day, calls $1 and $0.50 or 10 minutes a call. That is Tier 1 word for word.
  • You on Tier 1. About $5 of AI a month, so your answers would stop around the 19th. Your notes would still be kept and filed.
  • You on Tier 2. $8 covers your $5 month with room for personas and calls.
  • Testers. Their free codes stay as they are. Each tester's month shows which plan they really need.

7   What the pricing page would say

Plain claims that hold under the limits.

  1. FreeTry Zenod on your own notes. A new memory a day, a couple of answers a day and a weekly recap. Everything you send is kept.
  2. €9 a monthFor everyday use. About 4 new memories and 10 answers a day, a persona every day, desk jobs and 10 minutes of calls a month. Everything you send is kept.
  3. €19 a monthFor all-day use. About 10 new memories and 25 answers a day, three persona runs a day, 20 desk jobs a week and 30 minutes of calls a month. Everything you send is kept.

Each claim is the daily mix the plan's allowance pays for in full, so it holds even when someone uses every feature. "Everything you send is kept" is true today: filing never stops at the allowance. The €9 claim is true to the limits running now. Free and €19 become true with the change on the next slide.

8   What we recommend

Free, €9 as today, and €19.

Keep the €9 plan as Tier 1, add a free plan to start on and a €19 plan for heavy users. The free plan costs at most $1.10 a user a month. A €5 middle plan would halve every Tier 1 number and cut what today's payers bring in.

If you say yes, the team then does thisThe limits file gets one set of limits per plan instead of one for everyone, read from the account's subscription, with no subscription meaning Free. Stripe gets one new €19 monthly price next to today's €9. The pricing page shows the three cards. About two to three days of work. Nothing has been changed yet.
