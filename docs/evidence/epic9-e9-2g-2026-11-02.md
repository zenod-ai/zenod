## 1. What "Zenod council" was

- **The page.** It is `Zenot Council — Two Sub-Agents for Daily Voice Posts`, a design page from 3 July (GitHub issue #240 in the vault repo; "Zenot" is the voice-note spelling of Zenod). E9-2c filed it under the seeded root *Launching Epic Social*.
- **Why it looked high and fresh.** On 2 November at 03:10 an agent_team production-proof report ("E46-S9 … fake tester", source `mcp`) was stored as a capture. The librarian's agent-report filter (`isAgentOpsReport`) missed it, because the first line puts the ticket id in parentheses and the text has no telemetry word. E9-2b therefore filed it as a **provisional leaf** under the closest page, which was the council page. The Memory tab dates a node by the newest Log citation in its subtree and sorts children newest-first. One agent report was enough to date the council page 2026-11-02 and put it first under a working root.
- **Underneath,** freshness counted every capture. That includes agent reports, which are 110 of the 121 captures so far in November, and the `## Ops rollup` lines on root pages.
- **Fix.**
  - Every Log entry now has an origin (`vault/captureOrigin.ts`). WhatsApp, Telegram, web and Drive count as the person's. MCP and CLI notes count as the person's unless they read as agent reports (ticket or epic ids, run ids, commit SHAs next to deploy words, PR numbers, wake or Watcher reads). In Jordi's vault, 432 entries count as his and 166 as agent reports.
  - The Memory tab's *latest* and the discovery's heat use only the person's captures and ignore ops rollups. A page cited only by agent reports has no date.
  - After the re-root, the council page and its test leaf sit in long-term memory under the older Zenod engineering docs, not under a working root.

## 2. The method (code: `packages/core/src/engine/treeDiscover.ts`)

| Step | What | Why this and not something else |
|---|---|---|
| Topics | TF-IDF over each page's own words (title, summary, tags, headings, body; ops rollups, citations, spoken filler and ingest headers removed), plus a body wikilink (+0.2), a `parent:` to a real page (+0.25) and a capture filed on both (+0.1 each, up to 3). Average-linkage clustering at 0.13. | The librarian already tokenises this way. It is deterministic, free and private. Embeddings would add a provider call per page and send the whole vault out, for no gain at about 100 pages. Average linkage keeps one generic page from chaining everything together. |
| Seeded aggregators | The 11 E9-2c root pages are recognised by their boilerplate. Their seed summary, headings and "In this branch" links are ignored. They join the topic they read most like, or stand alone. | Otherwise the hand-picked seed keeps steering the new tree. |
| Freshness | Each of the person's own captures adds 0.5^(age/14 days) to the pages it is filed on. A capture that only an aggregator holds counts on the real page it reads most like (cosine at least 0.12), otherwise on the aggregator. | Agent reports never count (E9-A4). |
| Working vs long-term | Working at heat ≥ 0.9; stays working until below 0.3 (hysteresis); at most 8. The rest merge into at most 6 long-term areas, each of at least 3 pages. | The tree does not flap week to week. |
| Names | A root is a page of its own topic: the one the topic already hangs under, weighted by the person's captures and by a short title. Dates and subtitles are dropped. A root continuing from the last run keeps its page and name. | Names are his own page titles. Pin and rename are a follow-up (below). |
| Where it runs | The weekly sweep (E9-2d) re-derives the roots, repeating until nothing changes (at most 3 passes). By hand: `zenod tree discover [--apply] [--fresh]`. Vaults without Tree.md are untouched. | |
| What it writes | Tree.md (now `generated: zenod-discover`) plus `parent:` and `memory:` lines. It never moves, creates or deletes a file, and never touches `Log/` or `_attachments/`. | E9-A2 safety rules. |

Tests: `packages/core/test/treeDiscover.test.ts`, 10 tests on a made-up fixture vault:
- a hot topic is promoted under its own page name;
- the seeded root and the agent-report page do not become working roots;
- hysteresis: a cooling topic stays working, a cold one is not promoted, and a long-cold one drops;
- the first apply writes frontmatter only and keeps lint clean; a second run continues the same roots and writes nothing; revert restores the vault byte for byte;
- a tree-less vault is left alone;
- the sweep reports its counts;
- the Memory tab never dates a node from agent reports.
