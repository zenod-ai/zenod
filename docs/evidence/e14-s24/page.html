Helpers, one inbox, and a quick index

Bottom line. Personas become helpers. Each one reads your memory on its own schedule and leaves you suggestions you can act on with one tap in the one inbox: Do it, Change, or Not now, like Epic Team's review cards. A small quick index of your topics, people and nicknames sits in front of the chat, so “the flat” or “Ana's thing” lands on the right memory. On DeepSeek it costs about a third of a cent a day for a typical user and 1 to 2 cents for you. About 9 days of build in 6 small steps. We recommend yes.

Helpers, not personasThe same six, renamed everywhere you see them. Each says in one line what it does with your memory and what it may suggest.
Cards you can act onToday a card mostly tells you something. Now a helper can propose a change, and you do it, edit it first, or say not now. Every change can be undone.
Zenod knows what you meanA quick index of your places, people and nicknames, plus what is live this week, read before every chat answer.

How it fits together

Helpers never change your memory themselves. A change happens only when you press Do it, as one job on the memory desk that you can undo. Your words in the Log are never edited.

The quick index

What it is. One short page Zenod keeps about your memory: every place in your tree with a one-line summary, the names and nicknames you use for it (“the flat”, “my sister”, “the Laia”), how many notes and when you last mentioned it, plus a “this week” list of what is live. About 2,000 words for a memory like yours.

How it is built cheaply. Half of it exists already: the memory tree keeps a one-line summary for each place. New: after each note is filed, one small DeepSeek call updates only the places that note touched and picks up any new nickname. Once a night, one call rebuilds “this week”. The first build reads each place once.

How chat uses it. Before Look up or Talk it over answers, the index goes in front of the question, so the model can map “the flat” to Moving to Valencia and search there first. The reply shows a quiet “Read as” line, and you can tap it to correct it. Keep is unchanged: filing already places notes. WhatsApp and Telegram replies stay exactly as they are. This changes the web chat, calls and connected AI apps, because you asked for it in your own words.

How far Zenod is “EpicSpine with a memory skin”

Conceptually it holds well. Literally it does not, and should not.

What we say. Zenod runs the same discipline as EpicSpine: one written truth, small undoable changes, nothing changes without your yes, history that never changes. It is applied to your memory instead of to a project. We do not build EpicSpine inside Zenod, and Zenod does not depend on it. Each stays a product on its own, and they meet only through MCP, as you asked on 2 November.
