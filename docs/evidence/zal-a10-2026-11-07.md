## The three options (as shown on the page)

Current: "⚠️ Your voice note was transcribed, but Zenod has not returned a save receipt. The transcript is safely retained; retry after the connection is repaired cannot create a second memory."

1. "⚠️ I got your voice note, but Zenod couldn't save it, so it isn't in your memory yet. Please send it again in a few minutes."
   True with today's code: only the text changes.
2. "⚠️ Zenod couldn't save your voice note just now. I've kept it and will keep trying, and I'll reply "Saved" here once it's in."
   True only after a Phylax change that retries `capture_retry_pending` notes by itself and does not replay a cached error (the second open follow-up in the A8 evidence).
3. "⚠️ I couldn't save that voice note yet. It's kept safe, and I'll confirm here when it's saved."
   Shortest, mirrors "I’m still filing this memory". Same retry change as option 2.

Truth check: the brief asked for options saying the note "will be filed automatically". After #1499 the outage case no longer reaches this warning; the cases that still reach it are not retried automatically. Options 2 and 3 therefore ship only together with that retry fix; option 1 is the text-only change.
