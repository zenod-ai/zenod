Zenod as a company memory: who would buy it

Owners of 10 to 200 person teams who already talk on WhatsApp would likely buy it. Nothing today turns every employee's voice notes into one company memory. Test it free with one team for two weeks before building.

Who would buy it

Most likely: the owner or manager of a 10 to 200 person business whose people work away from a desk and already talk on WhatsApp: shops, restaurants, clinics, building and field crews, small agencies. Their good ideas and warnings are said out loud, then lost.

  • The gap is real. About 80% of workers have no desk, yet they get about 1% of business software investment (Emergence Capital, via this 2026 summary). 73% of frontline staff still fill in paper forms (YOOBIC).
  • Small firms are left out. Glean, the best-known company memory, reportedly needs about 100 seats and about $60,000 a year (Activepieces).
  • People already pay $6 to $30 a person each month for nearby tools: idea boxes, meeting notes, wikis (table below).
  • No proof yet that anyone pays for a voice mailbox that becomes a company memory. That is what the test below finds out.

How it differs

  • From Zenod for one person: many people speak into one memory. So it needs each note tagged with who said it, rules on who can read what, and a weekly "what is bubbling up" for the owner. Zenod today has one owner per memory.
  • From EpicTeam: EpicTeam does the work for a founder. Company memory only listens, sorts and surfaces. Later it could hand a ripe idea to EpicTeam. Jordi said this on 10 Nov: "maybe Zenod can be a dispatcher eventually ... dispatch this memory to agent team".

The smallest test, and our recommendation

Recommendation: no build now. Run one two-week test with one friendly team of 5 to 15 people, at no cost to them and almost no code. A yes starts it now. Setup takes the team about one day, so the Zenod tester work, objective one, stays first.

  1. On a yes, Jordi says in his reply which business he has in mind and asks its owner himself, because the team never contacts a company. The team sets up everything else.
  2. Each staff member gets a personal voice link, the one EpicTeam already uses for tester feedback. Their notes land in one Zenod memory set up for that business.
  3. Each Friday the owner gets one page: the top ideas, problems and repeats of the week, with who said them.
  4. After two weeks, three numbers decide it. Does at least half the staff send one note a week or more? Does the owner open the Friday page? Would the owner pay about €49 a month for a team under 20? That price is our guess.

If two of the three are yes, we build the three missing parts listed above. If not, we park the idea.
