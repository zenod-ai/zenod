## Why the read showed two

`listApplicants` linked an account to an applicant by `redeemedBy` or by `stripe_email`. A Stripe promotion code never set `redeemedBy` (only Zenod's own codes did), and the checkout email differed. So the account fell into `other_accounts`: 1 applicant (signed_up 0) + 1 other account.

## Fix: [#1665](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1665)

- `TesterFlow.linkStripeCode`: when an invite checkout carries a tester's Stripe code, the account is linked to that applicant (`redeemedBy`, first account only). This covers later testers whatever email they type.
- `listApplicants` also matches an account by its **verified** sign-in emails (customer identities). This fixes the 13 Nov account without editing production data.
- Tests: `packages/server/test/testerApplicants.test.ts` ("E26-S25: counts a tester who signed in with the form's email…": before = 1 applicant + 1 other; after = `1 applicant`, step `signed_up`; unverified email ignored; code link alone works) and `testerFlow.test.ts` (`linkStripeCode`).
- Live after the next production promote. Until then the production read still shows the split.
