Let’s start

✓ Your memory is connected

It lives in a Zenod folder in your Google Drive or GitHub, whichever you picked. You own it.

✓ Nothing to set up for AI

Zenod’s AI comes with your plan. Claude or Codex use your own account with them, as they do today.

    1. Open Settings › WhatsApp & Telegram and type your phone number.
    2. From that phone, send the code it shows to Zenod’s WhatsApp number.
    3. Send anything at all, a thought, a link, a photo.
    Ticks itself when your first message arrives and Zenod replies.
    2. Ticks itself when Claude or Codex first reads or saves through Zenod.

    1. Something you will want back next week. A name, a plan, a book someone recommended. Say it in WhatsApp, tell Claude “save this to Zenod”, or type it in Chat below.

      Ticks itself when Zenod has filed it. A voice note counts too.

    2. Ask the way you would ask a friend who was listening: “what was that book Ana mentioned?” Zenod answers with where it found it.

      Ticks itself when an answer brings back what you kept in step 3.

    3. Helpers read your memory while you sleep and leave you a short note in the Inbox. They all start off. Pick the one that sounds useful.

      Ticks itself when you flip any switch.
    Stuck on a step? Write to support@zenod.dev and a person helps.

    What I recommend

    • Step 5 becomes “switch one helper on”. Jordi’s words were switching one off, but testers start with all of them off, so turning one on is the only move that does anything. Off again counts too.
    • Nothing new in WhatsApp. The card lives in the app only. WhatsApp keeps replying “Saved” and nothing else.
    • Codex gets browser sign-in checked and a line on zenod.dev/connect, or step 2 says Claude first and Codex with a token.
    • The score lives in the app, in the header and as one card above Chat. It disappears at 5 of 5 or when hidden, and can come back from Settings.

    Size of the build: about one day. One small read of five facts Zenod already has, the card and the header score, a few tests. No change to filing, finding or chat replies.
