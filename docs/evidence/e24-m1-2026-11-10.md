# Zenod errors, 9 to 10 November: what we found and what changed

**Bottom line: 7 kinds of error found, 2 fixed live today, 2 were already handled, 2 need their own next step, 1 is noise. Our first fix caused a 7-minute crash loop this morning, now stopped.**

What you would have seen: notes from WhatsApp and Claude still got saved and filed, but filing often took a minute longer than it should. For a few seconds during each of last night's three releases, Zenod answered with an error. Between 04:00 and 04:07 UTC today Zenod restarted three times because of our fix, so anything you tried then may have failed once. The health page counted 229 error lines in the last 24 hours, almost all from the filing problem.

What changed, live on production since 04:07 UTC on 10 November:

- Filing runs on the main model again, the one that already finished the failed filings. No more failed first try and wasted minute.
- The cheaper model no longer thinks until it runs out of room, and one long internal note no longer throws away its whole answer. This stays in the code for when filing goes back to the cheaper model.

## Where we looked

- Every request through the front door to zenod.dev, cloud.zenod.dev and phylax.zenod.dev since 23:00 UTC on 9 November, 40,626 lines.
- The Zenod app's own log since 02:05 UTC on 10 November. Older app logs went away with the containers replaced at each release; the health page kept only their times.
- The WhatsApp companion's log since 16:22 UTC on 9 November.
- The health page's own error history for the full 24 hours, and the 15-minute self-tests, all passed.

Counts and kinds of error only. No message content is on this page.

### 1. Filing redone on the main model

Filing ran on DeepSeek's own service first and fell back to OpenRouter when that failed. DeepSeek thinks before it answers unless told not to, and the thinking shares its output allowance. With no setting, a filing call thought for about a minute, ran out at 8,192 tokens and failed. A second kind: the reconcile step rejected the whole answer when the model's note about why it chose something passed 240 characters. Both fell back to OpenRouter, which then filed the note, so nothing was lost, only time and a wasted call.

The code change turned thinking off on this route and cut an over-long note to 240 characters. It was released at 03:56 UTC and the first filing call afterwards worked: 2,569 tokens in 13.6 seconds, where before it was 8,192 tokens and a failure after 57 seconds.

### 2. The crash loop our fix caused

With DeepSeek now answering, its answer for one new note made Zenod use all of its memory about 10 seconds later, at the step after the classification. Zenod restarted, picked the same note up again and ran out of memory again, three times in 7 minutes. At 04:07 UTC we moved filing back to OpenRouter by a setting and restarted. The same note then filed in under 2 minutes and Zenod has stayed up since, using about 176 of its 320 MB.

Next step: find why one DeepSeek answer makes the filing step use unbounded memory, with a test that feeds it that shape of answer, then move filing back to the cheaper model.

### 3. Errors during a release

Each release stops the old app before the new one is up, so for 2 to 13 seconds every request gets an error. Fixing it means starting the new app before stopping the old one, which needs care because both would write the same memory files for a moment. Next step: a separate row to make releases seamless.

### 4. Slow search

One memory search took 0.4 seconds at 02:17 and 4 seconds at 02:30 on the same index, after new notes were filed. It holds up other requests while it runs: a health check took 4.3 seconds at that moment. The cause is in how the search index refreshes after a filing and needs profiling on a full-size test memory. Next step: a separate row.

## Proof after the release

- Code: the change was merged with green checks and released as d94ef24, live at 03:56:48 UTC on 10 November. Health reports d94ef24.
- Setting: filing moved to OpenRouter at 04:07:03 UTC; the app restarted at 04:07:04 and has had no crash since.
- After the setting: 1 note classified and reconciled on OpenRouter with no failed call, and its job finished at 04:10:02.
