3   What is the simple first version, and what comes later

Three plans now. Self-reflection next. To-dos and the bundle later.

  • Now: Self-hosted. Already on zenod.dev. Nothing to build.
  • Now: Zenod €9. Today's plan and price. Background moves to DeepSeek's own API, so the same $3 of AI buys 10 memories a day instead of 4. Needs one DeepSeek account and key, which is your call.
  • Now: Zenod Plus €19. One new Stripe price, a $10 AI allowance per Plus account, all six personas on daily, a pricing card. About 2 days.
  • Next: self-reflection. A nightly multi-step persona run for Plus that reads the week and writes back what it learned. About 1 to 2 days once Plus exists.
  • Later: to-do add-ins and an Epic Team starter bundle. The bundle would be a joint offer, with Zenod and Epic Team kept as separate products. Any build that crosses between them comes to you first with its own warning.

Left out on purpose. No hosted free plan, no MCP-only plan, no middle plan. A flat DeepSeek subscription such as OpenCode Go, where $10 buys $100 of use, would cut background about ten times more. It is sold for coding agents, so we price on the pay-as-you-go API and count the subscription only as upside.
