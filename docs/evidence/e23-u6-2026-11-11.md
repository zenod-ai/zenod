## Why it missed

Zenod's quick search list leaves out log entries that read as the agents' own work, like a self-test or a deploy note. That keeps agent chatter out of your answers. Search still found the self-test by its marker word, because no other memory matched it and the slower full scan took over. Ask searches in plain words. Other memories matched, so the full scan never ran and the entry never showed up.

## What changed

- Ask's own search now adds up to three of those left-out log entries after your own matches, and only ones that contain the question's words.
- Search, the app chat and chat replies are unchanged. Filing, page format and the tree are unchanged.
- The standard question set has a new check. It saves a self-test memory, waits for search to find it, then asks about it in plain words. Ask must find it. It runs on every change, nightly and on every production roll.
