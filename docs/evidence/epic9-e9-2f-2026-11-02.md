## The problem

E9-3c's eval Q7 found the right answer but cited it one level too shallow. The answer's Log entry was cited only by a level-1 root page, because the E9-2c clean-up had re-filed it as a dated line on the root. The deeper page that covers the entry did not cite it, and retrieval lineage (`placedCitersOf`, deepest citer first) can only name pages that cite the entry.

## What shipped (fix at the filing side)

- **Weekly sweep, new step** (`deepenRootCitations` in `packages/core/src/engine/treeSweep.ts`). A Log entry cited only by level-1 roots (outside `## Ops rollup`) is also cited in the `## Captures` section of the deepest page under that root that matches it: `- <date> · <one-line summary> [[<date>#^e-…]]`. Matching reuses E9-2b's `chooseTreeNode` with a new `under: <root>` restriction. The root keeps its citation, nothing is removed, and entries that no deeper page fits stay as they are.
- **Librarian (E9-2b).** Before this change, `placeCapture` only set parents and never added a deeper citation. Now a filing that reached only level-1 roots, and is not an ops rollup, also gets the leaf citation and its own `filed:` line.
- **Stricter bar than placing a page** (`citeOnLeaf`). The leaf's own name must match a word that the root's name and summary do not already carry, and the score must be at least 3 (a name word plus one more). Agent ops reports stay on the root (rule 5).
  - A private dry run on a clone of the real vault showed why the bar is needed. With the plain E9-2b bar, matching against the whole Log entry, about 30 unrelated captures went to one broad page because of a single shared word. With the final bar, 9 of 74 root-only entries are deepened, each onto a page whose own name matches, and Q7's entry lands on its expected page.
- **Same safety as the sweep.** No write under `Log/` or `_attachments/`, tree lint may not get worse, and all changes go into ONE vault commit (`memory: weekly tree sweep (… deepened N …)`). New counts: `rootOnlyCitations`, `citationsDeepened`. Doctrine: `docs/LIBRARIAN-DOCTRINE.md`.
