## Findings

- **R5-F1. A paid answer is thrown away when a memory read meets a busy vault (it costs the user money).** In the cap run, 30 of 30 MCP `chat_with_zenod` answers came back as "Memory filing is in progress. Please retry your question shortly." Each was still billed about 10 ¢, about $2.40 in all. The run used 2 to 3 calls in parallel, as an agent might. In `packages/core/src/engine/engine.ts:3727`, `finalize` swaps the model's finished answer for that line when any read hit `VaultWriteBusyError`, after the model call was paid for. The busy cause is likely each concurrent chat's own write, or filing. In the app, single chats answered normally. **This needs a fix row:** either do not charge for a discarded answer, or wait for the read and retry it before answering. The cap still held, but a real tester's agent could burn most of the $3 on non-answers.
- **R5-F2. `ask_brain` returned `unstructured_error` once.** It happened at 04:10:24Z, after 70 s, right after the saves finished, and the same question worked 50 s later. It was probably the same busy window as R5-F1, so watch it rather than fix it now.
- **R5-F3. At the cap, the chat reply does not say the note was kept.** "Please keep a note: …" at the cap got only "This month's AI allowance … is used up". The note was in fact kept and filed, as Usage & plan promises. A tester would think it was lost. A one-line copy change fixes it ("Your note is kept"). A question sent at the cap is also kept as a note.
- **R5-F4. The Usage & plan headline shows "75 % used" while answers are paused.** The headline averages AI (100 %) with messages and voice (0 %). The line below it says "this month's allowance is used up", so the two read as a contradiction. The spend line says "$3.18 of $3.00", a 6 % overrun from in-flight calls and filing past the allowance (by design).
- **R5-F5. Saves are slow.** Each MCP save took about 1.5 to 2 min, run one after another, so 3 saves took 5.5 min. The chat save answered at once ("Filing it…"). The voice leg's "Saved" replies may come late in the same way. This is worth timing in the WhatsApp leg.
- Housekeeping: Xvfb and Chromium for this round were stopped at 04:22Z. The `operations-profile` and its Google session stay. The test account is now paused for AI until 2026-12-01, and saving and voice still work. The run's scratch files (`/var/lib/vps3-scratch/t3agent/t7r5`) hold scripts and screenshots, no secrets.
