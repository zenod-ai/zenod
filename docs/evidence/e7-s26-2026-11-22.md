## Who gets it, and who does not

- A real tester whose WhatsApp is verified on that account, linked for 24 hours or more, with no WhatsApp note sent and no memory saved.
- Not our own test accounts, not an account whose WhatsApp is not linked, not a person who already sent a note, not the same person twice (kept per account and per email address).
- The 21 Nov tester (linked 06:05Z on 22 Nov, no note) is the first one it would reach, the moment you say yes.
- If a send fails it is tried again on the next 15-minute pass, three times at most. Each message has a fixed id that the WhatsApp service remembers, so a retry cannot send it twice.

## How it is built

Zenod already sends one fixed-text WhatsApp message to a tester at the link (E26-S26) and a fixed billing notice, both through the same Phylax account notice. This adds a third fixed text there, so nothing can send a free-form message. A new timer in Zenod checks every 15 minutes, and while the switch is off it does nothing at all (no reads, no sends), so it adds nothing to the CPU work of M34.

- Switch: `POST /api/ops/testers/first-note-nudge {"mode":"on"}` with the ops token, or `ZENOD_TESTER_FIRST_NOTE_NUDGE=on`. Default off. The same route with `off` stops it before the next message.
- `GET .../due` lists the masked accounts that would get it now and sends nothing.
- Tests: 35 passing across the nudge, the welcome and the onboarding files, including: off sends nothing, once per account and per address, test accounts left out, a note stops it, the 24 hours counted from the first time the link was seen when it was not watched, a retry cap of three, and the text under 50 words.
- The Phylax companion has to run this change before the switch goes on, because the notice kind is new there. On a Yes the team deploys it, checks `due`, switches on and reports what was sent.

## What changes on a Yes

The team switches the nudge on. The 21 Nov tester gets this one message, and later testers get it once, 24 hours after they link. On a No the code stays off and nothing is sent.
