# E24-M36 evidence: a long WhatsApp voice note gets a reply (2026-11-23)

Objective: Zenod: Get 5 testers: a tester who sends a long voice note on WhatsApp must get a reply, not silence.

**Answer: long notes are now written out for free by the central service on vps3 in parts, with a time limit that grows with the note, and the owner is told "about N min" at once. Live on Phylax since 17:12Z; three real queued notes went through it within 6 minutes (818 s in 233 s).**

```
BEFORE  note over 90 s ─▶ central skipped ─▶ OpenAI 429 "no credits" ─▶ whisper.cpp base, 1 thread ─▶ minutes to hours, one note at a time, others queue behind it
AFTER   note over 90 s ─▶ cut at pauses into ~5 min parts ─▶ central service (small), one part after another ─▶ saved reply
                          └ one plain reply at once: "Long voice note, transcribing it, about N min."
        central fails ─▶ OpenAI ─▶ whisper.cpp, as before     health row 19 red on OpenAI "no credits"
```

## The change (PR [#1754](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1754), merge `bbbd589`, ci green; PR [#1755](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1755))

1. **Long job on the central service** (`centralTranscription.ts`, `transcribe.ts`): over 90 s the note is cut at the middle of the pause nearest each 5 minutes (ffmpeg silencedetect; at 5 minutes when there is no pause), as 16 kHz mono 32 kb/s MP3 parts of about 1.2 MB, sent one after another on `small`. Time limit: 120 s plus 0.6 s per second of audio (7086 s: 72 min); a part waits at most 4 min; a part that finds the service down or restarting is tried once more after 10 s; a silent part adds no words; any failure continues to OpenAI and whisper.cpp exactly as before, with the same single `central_fallback` alarm. One part holds the service about a minute, so other callers' short notes slip in between (below).
2. **One plain reply at once** (`phylaxPortedRuntime.ts`): before this a voice note got "I received your voice note and queued it for transcription. Transcription is usually quick; ..." whatever its length, then nothing until the saved reply. A note over 3 minutes now gets instead, once: "Long voice note, transcribing it, about N min. I will reply here once it is saved. Send “cancel transcription” to stop it." (N = 0.3 x length in minutes, rounded up: 10 min about 4, 60 min about 20, 2 h about 36). The saved reply is the existing one.
3. **Health** (`docs/health/s1-live`): `credit_event` turns an OpenAI HTTP 429 with "no credits remaining" or `insufficient_quota` into a credit refusal; row 19 is now "AI credit: DeepSeek, OpenRouter and OpenAI" and goes red for 48 h after one (the paid fallback has no balance to read). A long note's stated minutes plus 5 are added to the 15 min and 60 min "no reply" windows (`[voice] long_note secs=<n> eta_min=<n>` line), so a working 60 minute note does not turn row 14 red. Tests: `test_probe.py` (the real 23 Nov line, rate limit, timeout, other provider, `shared_stt.usage` are not refusals), `test_aggregate.py` (red 48 h, older green, long-note windows on rows 4 and 14); server: `centralTranscription.test.ts` (28), `phylaxChannels.test.ts`.
4. **A stop no longer fails a note** (PR #1755, found live): the deploy at 17:12Z aborted the 2 h transcription; the transcriber answers an abort with a failure receipt, so the worker marked the note failed (a test fails without the fix: state `failed`, not `queued`). See the 7086 s note below.

## The 7086 s (2 h) note: did it ever get a reply?

**No transcript, and probably a failure message.** whisper.cpp base at one thread ran from 15:26Z to the deploy at 17:13Z (1 h 47 min) without finishing. The old container logged `transcription.failed reason timeout` at 17:13:34Z when the deploy stopped it; the new container never processed a 7086 s note (the central service logged none), though a requeued job would have been claimed first (oldest first). So the stop turned it into a failed note and the owner most likely got "Zenod could not finish transcribing that voice note. Please try a shorter note or try again." at 17:13:40Z (a message was delivered then; the text is not readable from the log). That is the bug PR #1755 fixes: a stop now requeues. The audio stays in the owner's custody; sending it again now takes about 36 minutes on the central service, and the owner is told so at once. I did not re-trigger it (a real person's note).
