## Where people drop, and why

1. **WhatsApp linking for any new account (the drop that lost the one real tester).** zenod-mt logged `[ops-alert] app client_api … api=/api/channels/whatsapp/challenge status=503` at 2026-11-13 20:47:54Z. Phylax's management MCP answers the challenge with `runtime.whatsapp.status().linkedNumber`, and `WhatsAppGateway.status()` masks it (`••••1234`, `maskPhoneNumber`). zenod-mt's `projectHostedChannelAction` only accepts `/^\+?[\d ()-]{8,32}$/` for `challenge.sharedNumber`, returns null, and the BFF answers 503 "Channels are temporarily unavailable". Phylax had already registered the phone, hence `pending`. A masked number could not be messaged anyway. The hosted channel tests faked `status()` with an unmasked number, so CI never failed. The T7 rehearsal left WhatsApp to the first real tester (E7-A23), so production never ran this path before.
2. **On a phone, the home had no way to the tester page before the footer.** At 390 px the header nav is hidden and the home passed no menu. The first `/testers` link sat at y≈18,160 of 18,665 px. "Get started" goes to the 9 euro pricing.
3. **The tester page works.** It states the three promises (EPIC-26), and the form answers on screen. Applying to a 0 euro account took the stranger 3 minutes.
4. **Very little traffic.** 10 post visits in 2.5 days. One personal Reddit reply (P-0219, r/ClaudeAI, to someone giving up on Claude as a personal assistant) brought 2 visits and the applicant. Broadcast posts (P-0209 build-in-public, P-0191 YouTube Short, P-0206) brought 8 visits and no applicants.
