    How we would build it

    Round 1's plan stands: three of the four modes already come out of today's Send decision, and Talk it over is new, about half of a week's work. Round 2 adds the regrouping and the charts. The regrouping moves panels that exist into Memory and Settings, and old links like ?tab=graph keep working. The charts are new. Together that is about two more days.

    Kept as it is: WhatsApp replies do not change, and how memories are filed and found does not change.

    Then we check it: on a fixed set of your real messages, Zenod has to pick the right mode at least 9 times in 10, and “who are you?” has to get a direct answer.
