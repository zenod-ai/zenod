## Why the health row was red

The rule (ZAL-A8, A12) turns the Phylax row red when a WhatsApp message gets no "Saved" for an hour, unless a chat job finished within 5 min after it (a question). It learned "a chat job finished" only from `done: chat` lines in the zenod-mt containers Docker keeps (5). Production rolled many times overnight, so the 18:50:22 answer and the 03:15–03:18 answers dropped out of the logs and the rule flagged them.
