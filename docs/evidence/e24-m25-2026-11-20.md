## Why the vault was not recovered here

Recovery has to either (a) move or rewrite the conflict marker and the journal in the tenant's Google Drive, or (b) let `recoverTransactions` run on, which includes `git reset --hard` and, with no local clone, `rm -rf` of the working copy. With the external edit unseen and the unpublished commit possibly the only copy of someone's notes, either step is the destructive step on a real user's vault the brief says to stop for. No code was written that touches a vault.
