Tester onboarding: one WhatsApp message and one thank-you email

When a new tester links WhatsApp, Zenod sends at once one WhatsApp message with a link to a 60-second video of five things to try, and one thank-you email from you with the call offer inside. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

Mock-ups: exactly what the tester gets

The five things to try first, all live on production today

  1. Drop a voice noteon WhatsApp, the way you'd leave yourself one. Zenod answers Saved.
  2. Find your own copyword for word, in your Google Drive or GitHub, in the Log folder.
  3. Ask it backin the app's chat: "what did I say about…?"
  4. Use it from Claude or ChatGPTadd Zenod once at zenod.dev/connect, then ask there.
  5. See your memory graphyour notes as topics and links, in the app.

Helpers are left out: testers start with them off, and the video only shows what works for them on day one.

The video: 60 seconds, seven frames, at zenod.dev/start

How it is made: these seven frames are the base. After your yes the team animates them in the house paper style with the brand zenod, a local voice and captions, as real screen captures from a test account. No paid service. Vertical 9:16 for phones. The page zenod.dev/start holds the video and, under it, the WhatsApp how-to in four lines.

The changes: where each piece starts, what it costs, how it stops

Four small builds inside Zenod, one switch for all of them.

WhatsApp messageStarts where Zenod confirms a new WhatsApp link, the first time only: packages/server/src/phylaxUnit.ts, verifyInbound. The confirmation reply gets the new text. Repeat codes send nothing new. This is a chat change, so it ships only on your yes in your own words (rule L-6). It rolls the WhatsApp companion once.
Thank-you emailSame moment, same place, queued once per account through Zenod's mailer (welcomeMail.ts, built in E26-T2, off today). It replaces the "You're in" welcome after sign-up (zenodUnit.ts, onCheckoutWelcome), so a tester gets one mail, not two.
SenderJordi at Zenod <jordi@zenod.dev>, replies to jordi@zenod.dev. Zenod's sender is built, but no sender is switched on in production today: the Cloudflare route was parked when you said no more keys. The build switches on the Google route Zenod already has, which sends from your own mailbox. It needs that mailbox connected once: one Allow from you at cloud.zenod.dev/admin/mail, about a minute, no key; the build's card brings the link. Until then the email waits in Zenod's queue and the WhatsApp half runs alone.
zenod.dev/startA new page on the site: the video plus a four-line WhatsApp how-to. The video file is served by the site, so it streams in the browser.
Cost0 euro a tester. The WhatsApp message goes from the number Zenod already runs; the email from your mailbox, inside Google's free daily limit. The video is made with the team's own render tool.
Switch offOne setting, ZENOD_TESTER_ONBOARDING=0, stops both the next minute: the WhatsApp reply goes back to today's text, queued emails are dropped. Mails already sent cannot be taken back.

Implications

  • Every tester gets a message and a mail in your name, so their replies come to jordi@zenod.dev and someone has to answer them.
  • "A quick call" is a promise: you or the team takes the call when a tester replies "call".
  • Only testers who link WhatsApp get the email. Someone who signs up and stops before linking gets nothing; that drop shows on your per-tester WhatsApp report (E26-S21).

Our recommendation

Yes, for every new tester, as shown: one WhatsApp message and one email at the moment WhatsApp links, the call offer inside the email, no day-3 mail. About two days of work: the video, the start page, the message and the mail. The first one goes to a test account of ours, and you see it before any tester does.

Do we send this to every new tester?

  1. Yes: build and switch it onThe team builds it, shows you the video and a test send, then switches it on. Recommended.
  2. Change it: tell us whatSay what to change in the text, the features or the timing. We redo this page.
  3. Not now: park itTesters keep today's flow: the verified reply and no email.
