## Research (Cloudflare docs, read 2026-11-12)
- Cloudflare Email Service sending: public beta since 2026-04-16, still labelled Beta. It sends by Workers binding, by REST (`POST /client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/email/sending/send`, with `from` {address, name}, `to`, `subject`, `text`, `html`, `reply_to`) or by SMTP (beta, 2026-06).
- Sending to any recipient needs **Workers Paid** ($5 a month, 3,000 emails a month included, then $0.35 per 1,000). Workers Free only sends to verified destination addresses.
- Limits: 50 recipients per message, 25 MiB, a conservative daily quota for new accounts that grows.
- Domain onboarding adds MX, SPF and DKIM on `cf-bounce.<domain>` plus DMARC. The docs describe it in the dashboard, and the API has `/zones/{zone}/email/sending/subdomains` (Zone Settings permission). The token needs **Email Sending: Edit** (account).
- Receiving: Email Routing is free with up to 200 rules and destination addresses. Those are forwarding addresses, not inboxes. zenod.dev's MX points at Google (smtp.google.com), so routing on zenod.dev itself would take over Jordi's Google inbox. A side name (for example team.zenod.dev) or Google aliases are the safe way to get "10 mailboxes" as addresses.
- Compared with Amazon SES (about $0.10 per 1,000, but a new AWS account and sandbox exit), Resend and Postmark (outside accounts, Resend refused in E26-A4), and the Google one-click consent from round 2 (free, kept as a fallback, withdrawn as an ask in E26-A9).
