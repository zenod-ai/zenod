## 3. Change

[#1703](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1703):
- `packages/server/src/phylaxSharedTranscription.ts`: the monthly meter. It adds the estimated cost of every billed OpenAI attempt (0.003 USD per audio minute), closes the route at `PHYLAX_SHARED_STT_MONTHLY_USD` (10) and opens it again when the UTC month turns. It survives restarts and logs one `shared_stt.usage` line per attempt.
- `phylaxUnit.ts`: tenants on `local` or the platform OpenRouter path go to OpenAI first on the shared key. On any error they fall back to local whisper.cpp, never to the 402 OpenRouter key. A tenant that brought its own Groq or OpenAI key keeps it. `transcription.done` now carries `shared_openai`.
- `transcribe.ts`: an `openaiModel` per call, and notes go in 5-minute parts in parallel, because gpt-4o transcribe models cap their output.
- Off switch: `PHYLAX_SHARED_STT=0`, or remove the key.

Tests: `packages/server/test/phylaxSharedTranscription.test.ts` covers the cap, a restart, the month turning, routing, a 610 s note sent in 3 parts, and a forced OpenAI 500 falling back to whisper.cpp.
