## Where the alert goes (and why)

- **Team: the Zenod desk.** `desk_note.py daily` (agent_team E96-S53, the shape our own checks use) writes one note a day, key `tester-stuck`, in this repo's `desk/inbox/`. New stuck testers are added as lines, and a tester already reported keeps its line. The console puts open desk notes in the Zenod Delivery Manager's wake prompt, so the next wake acts on it. That is the path that wakes the Delivery Manager. An ops-alert line would reach Jordi too, which this row rules out unless it is our bug.
- **Jordi: only our bug.** A step that is our failure also goes out as one `whatsapp-notify alert` (kind `zenod-tester-stuck-our-bug`, capped by the console at 1 per kind per 12 h and 3 a day). It is sent on a short console run of the Zenod Delivery Manager, the persona that holds the skill and is placed on "Five testers use Zenod". This is how the existing ops alarms reach him. Not fired today: nobody is stuck on our bug now. The path is covered by `check.test.py`, and the live alarm was deliberately not tested because it would have messaged Jordi.
