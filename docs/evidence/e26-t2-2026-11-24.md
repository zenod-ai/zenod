## Why T2 folds (and into S33, not S28)

- **Same moment.** The tester sign-up is a Stripe Checkout with the tester coupon at €0 (`customerLayer.ts`, the invite branch with `testerCoupon`). Its completion is both T2's trigger and the "free sign-up finished" that queues E26-S33. The thank-you (E26-S28) goes later, when WhatsApp is linked, so T2 does not overlap it. The brief said "thank-you after sign-up"; in the code the sign-up email is E26-S33.
- **Same person, same job.** Both say "you're in, here is what to try first, reply to me". S33 is live, its text was approved word for word (EPIC-6-A4, Jordi 15 Nov), and it has already gone to all three testers.
- **One extra path, and it does not need T2 switched on now.** T2 fires on every Stripe checkout. Strangers can reach the tester checkout, so public checkout is on (`checkoutEnabled` needs `ZENOD_PUBLIC_PAID_SIGNUP=1` in live mode; the name is set on zenod-mt, its value was not read). That means someone could pay €9 without a tester code. S33 only goes to testers, so that person would get no welcome email. No such customer exists today: 3 sign-ups, all testers. T2 as built cannot cover that case alone. Switched on, it also mails every tester a second welcome at the same moment, and it needs Zenod's own Google sender, which Jordi never connected. The fix for paying non-testers is a small new build: S33's queue with a non-tester text, sent by the same team Gmail timer. It is parked until the first paying stranger signs up, and the follow-up is on the row.
- **Different sender.** T2 needs Zenod's own Google sender, which Jordi never connected. All live tester mail goes through the team Gmail connection instead.
