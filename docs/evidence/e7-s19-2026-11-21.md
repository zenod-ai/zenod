## Two things to know

- **The button opens the app, not WhatsApp directly.** The WhatsApp link carries a one-time code that Zenod makes for each tester when the app opens (E7-S16), so the email cannot hold it. One tap in the mail lands on the first screen, where Open WhatsApp is the first thing to press. A direct link would need a per-tester code made before the email goes; that is a separate build.
- **The notes line is sent to everyone, not only those who have not done it.** We cannot tell from the queue whether a tester has connected a notes place, and the words are harmless to someone who has ("If it asks…"). The memory line in the app already says "a Zenod folder in your Google Drive or GitHub", so the words match what he sees.
