## Fix (PR [#1724](https://github.com/zenod-ai/zenod/pull/1724), squash `8079bd06`, ci green)

When the head is published, the only block is pending receipts and every receipt is unreadable (no replay possible), recovery calls `setAsideAbandonedChanges`: behind any live write and under the vault lock, `git stash push --include-untracked`, then `refs/zenod/set-aside/<time>-<sha>` pointing at that commit (so a stash drop or gc cannot lose it), the working tree returns to the published head. Never touches a commit, the remote or a note; anything else (a readable receipt, an unpublished head, a clean tree) is left to the old path. Tests: `vaultRecovery.test.ts` 6 of 6 (the killed-sweep state end to end fails without the change; the not-eligible cases keep their files), `tsc` clean. Gate for production: demo 96/100/1.7, real notes 100/91.2/1.6.
