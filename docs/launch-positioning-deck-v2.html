Mission · North star

Maximize the share of your life's work that compounds — under your own keys.

  • Knowledge compounds only if it's filed, cited, and owned — not scattered across ten vendors' databases.
  • Work compounds only if every outcome becomes memory the next task can stand on.
  • Attention compounds only if it's guarded — every interruption spent is compounding you gave away.
The metric we maximize: how much of what you and your agents do today makes tomorrow cheaper — without renting your mind to anyone.
The problem

Today, you are the context bus.

You re-teach every agent, you carry context between them, you triage every ping. Ten agents means ten amnesiac contractors — and one exhausted coordinator.

What's new

The shift: from context bus to ring of gatekeepers.

A gatekeeper is an agent whose job is to say no on your behalf — so everything else can safely say yes.
The Council

Six governors. Each a domain expert. One table.

Zenod is not the boss. The power is the table, not any one seat.

The powerful idea

The Council is itself an MCP server.

Point any harness at one endpoint and it inherits your whole operation: authenticated, trained on you, and gated. It never gets the keys — it gets an audience.

Trained on you

One memory they all share.
Every correction trains the whole ring.

  • Trained on you, by you, over time. Every preference you state, every correction you make, gets filed — and every governor reads the same pages.
  • Tasks recruit the governors they need. A voice note may touch three of them; you never route it yourself.
Philosophy

The Council are GitHub maximalists.

  • No proprietary database. The substrate is repos you already own. Leave anytime; take everything, with history.
  • Human and agent writing always distinguishable. Provenance is first-class; discipline is code, not willpower.
Principles

Five rules the whole suite obeys

Everybody reads. One writes. Each domain has exactly one gatekeeper with write authority. Agents ask; they never get raw CRUD.
Discipline as code, not willpower. Schemas, filing rules, and state machines are enforced deterministically — not in prompts.
You hold the keys. Plain markdown, your git, your server, your API keys. Export with zero loss.
Auditable to the commit. Every memory, ticket, and send is a commit or labeled event.
Outward needs your word. PRs, tweets, emails gate on human approval. Internal artifacts flow autonomously.

Positioning

Two landing pages. One story.

Never make one page do both jobs: a suite pitch with one shipped component reads as vaporware; a librarian pitch that buries the Council wastes the real story.

Honesty layer

What we say before HN says it for us

  • "Another multi-agent framework?" No framework to learn. It's a running household: opinionated governors over your assets, not a toolkit.
  • "Too early?" Correct, and we say so: we are user #1, M0 shipped with 48 deterministic tests. The vault is plain markdown — betting early costs nothing to exit.
  • "Why not Mem0 / Letta?" Their memory lives in their database. Yours lives in your git repo, with the audit trail.
  • "Greek names are a lot." Flavor, not homework. You only ever talk to one door: the Council.
