First, you two agree on the briefing.

herald

He doesn't start on vibes. He negotiates a briefing with you — a goal and a contract — and iterates it until you approve. That document is the first page of the memory.

every morning · the ritual

Ten proposals. You react.

herald · 9:00
Morning — ten proposals from what you've fed me. #3 comes from Tuesday's voice note, #7 riffs on the roadmap slide, #9 has an image I made for it.
you
✓ 1, 3 and 9. The rest: less hype, more of the memory angle. Give me five more.
herald
Five more, sharper. And your comment is filed — tomorrow's ten start from it.

You're not writing. You're reacting — and every reaction becomes memory. Week by week, more of his proposals ship untouched.

all day · the loop closes

Posted. Answered. Learned.

  • Approved posts go out, paced through the day — never a burst, never silence.
  • He works the threads — replies in your project's voice, grounded in what it actually knows.
  • He files what the audience taught him — what got replies, what got questions, what got nothing.
  • Tomorrow's ten proposals are born already knowing it. That's the loop. It never stops compounding.

And you can watch it: his scorecard shows proposals approved untouched climbing week over week (31% → 84%), and progress against the goal in the briefing.
