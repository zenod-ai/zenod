# Low-hanging fruit for the live apps/site

Ranked by benefit per effort. Nothing here is applied (apps/** is the deploy gate). Line numbers are against `main` at e722c85. Effort: S under 30 min, M a few hours.

1. **Drop the side-by-side chapter grid.** `apps/site/src/index.css:1197-1222` (the `@media (min-width: 1080px)` block) puts heading and diagram in two columns, and the diagram PNGs then render at about 55% width with 12px text. Delete the grid rules so each chapter is one column: heading, then diagram at full width. This alone answers "too many columns" and "diagrams show too little". Effort S.
2. **Remove the three-column caption row under every diagram.** `apps/site/src/components/story-sections.tsx:327-337` renders `.zls-captions` (CSS `index.css:1130-1150`, `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, ...)`). The captions repeat what the image says. Delete the block, or keep only the first caption as one sentence. Effort S.
3. **Cut 10 chapters to about 5.** `story-sections.tsx:41-285` (`storyChapters`). Merge `context`+`cultivate`+`originals` (same idea: keep originals, connect meaning), drop `cultivate` and `freedom` as separate screens, fold `alexandria` into one sentence in the footer or `agents` chapter. Ids to keep: `journey`, `recall`, `agents`, `start`, `build-your-library`. Update nav anchors at `App.tsx:140-144` to match. Effort M (copy merge), S if you only delete.
4. **Replace three or four heavy diagrams with one plain sentence or a photograph.** The chapters `cultivate` (`story-sections.tsx:93`), `freedom` (`:196`), `alexandria` (`:219`) and `options` (`:242`) are the densest. Remove the `<figure>` for these (`:303-350`, add a `chapter.image ? ... : null` guard) and let the lead sentence stand. Generated stand-ins are in `docs/landing-beta/img/`. Effort S.
5. **Remove the monospace numbered eyebrows.** `story-sections.tsx:292-294` renders "01 Good access starts with..." in tracked uppercase mono (`index.css:1082-1094`, `.zls-eyebrow`). The hero has the same pattern (`App.tsx:348`, `.v5-eyebrow`, `index.css:468-475`) and section tags `.v5-section-no`/`.v5-micro` (`index.css:435-460, 756`). Delete the `<p className="zls-eyebrow">` and fold the kicker into the lead. This is the biggest "feels like AI" tell. Effort S.
6. **Delete the hero proof strip and identity tag.** `App.tsx:367-390` (`.v5-identity`, `.v5-proof`: "Yours / MARKDOWN + GIT", "Portable / MODEL INDEPENDENT" ...; CSS `index.css:617-661`). Four tiny mono labels in a bordered 4-column row. Move the facts into the hero sentence ("plain Markdown you own, any model, any MCP client"). Effort S.
7. **Drop the two-colour split in every headline.** `story-sections.tsx:296-300` with `index.css:1104-1106` (`.zls-heading h2 > span { color: cyan }`) and the hero `v5-cyan`/`v5-acid` lines (`App.tsx:350-352`). One colour per headline reads calmer. Effort S.
8. **Rewrite the lines that read as AI.** All in `story-sections.tsx` leads and the hero. Examples: "It stores. It categorizes. It connects." (`:46`, forced triad); "Your librarian. Not your library." (`:56`, contrast reveal); "No model lock-in. No Zenod lock-in." (`:198`); "Return to what you actually said—not just a plausible answer about it." (`:149`, em dash plus not-X-but-Y); "Your memory, yours to contribute to, enrich, access, and cultivate." rolling verbs (`App.tsx:280-305`, `MemoryRoll`, used at `:364`; the SlotText roll is also a gimmick, consider removing). Plain replacements are in `docs/landing-beta/a/index.html`. Effort S.
9. **Collapse the pricing cards to a text block.** `App.tsx:215-268` and `index.css:738-780, 944` (`.v5-plans`, bordered cards with a mono tag). Two short paragraphs with the price set large, no card chrome, reads less templated, and the duplicate "offer truth" paragraph (`App.tsx:269`) can go because the cards already say it. Effort S.
10. **Shrink the image-link and caption chrome.** `story-sections.tsx:303-350`: each figure is wrapped in a "View full-size diagram" link plus a note line. Keep the notes only where they carry a real caveat (`journey` WhatsApp vs Telegram, `agents` MCP client requirement) and drop the link text. Effort S.
