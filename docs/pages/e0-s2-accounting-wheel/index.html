The smallest piece that turns it once

Your yes means one page a month: revenue, cost and margin per tester. A read-only monthly script in Zenod writes it. It takes revenue from Zenod's own record of paying accounts, AI cost from each account's usage record and server bills from the mailbox through the books reader. It moves no money and needs no new key.
