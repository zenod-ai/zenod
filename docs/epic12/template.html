Zenod vision deck, 7 November 2026

Your memory, open on a chat.

Ask it like WhatsApp. Watch the memories it used light up.

A plan for Jordi to approve. Every screen here is a mockup with invented demo data. Nothing on it is built yet unless a slide says so.

1   The vision

The best place to keep your memory is one you can see into.

Today you send to Zenod and it files. Next, you open it and talk to what you kept.

  • Chat firstSign in and the first thing is a chat box that answers from your memory, with links.
  • A graph you walkEvery memory is a node, every link an edge. Open one, follow its links.
  • Retrieval that lights upAsk a question and five memories light up, each with a match score, in under a second. Same over MCP.
  • Digests itselfEach new memory is filed at once and the whole graph is tidied every night.

6   How self-digestion runs

Filed on arrival, tidied every night.

{{FLOW}}
Already runningFiling on capture and the search index both work today. Topics are re-found once a week.
What changesThe sweep moves from weekly to nightly and also rebuilds the link graph. Topics still come from your own notes, never from a seeded list.
What it costsThe sweep calls no AI model, so nightly costs only server time. Filing cost is unchanged.

8   What could go wrong

Four risks we plan for from the start.

PrivacyThe graph and chat show a whole vault to its owner only. The public page uses invented demo data, never a real vault.
Big vaultsJordi’s vault already indexes over 700 items. The graph opens on topics and expands on click so it stays readable at thousands.
CostEach chat answer costs about as much as an ask today, measured at a fraction of a cent. Search and the sweep use no model.
Vector searchKeyword search already finds the right page. We measure misses first and add vectors only if they beat it, since vectors send vault text to a provider.

9   What we propose next

Approve this plan, and step 1 starts.

Ranked retrieval with a match score, on MCP first. It is the engine the chat and the lit-up graph both run on.

How you will check itAsk Zenod a question from Claude. Five memories come back, each with a percentage and a link that opens it, in under a second. After that, the chat-first landing.
