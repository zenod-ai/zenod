why he gets smarter

He keeps notes.
In a memory you own.

  • Everything he learns lands in your project's memory — voice, positioning, what worked, what the audience taught him.

  • Fire him anytime; keep everything he learned. The memory is yours the day you leave. Try that with an agency.
