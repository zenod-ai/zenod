# The Council — Naming & Roster (draft for revision)

Status: **draft, for later revision.** Captures the 2026-06-30 naming landing point so it
stops living only in chat. Supersedes the scattered "Console / Board / Hetairoi" framing.
The product-name decision record stays in the brain at `Projects/Zenod/Naming.md`
(obsidian-brain); this file governs the *team* layer.

## The landing point

**It's your memory and your team.** You talk to the Council. Six governors keep your world;
you make the calls.

- **The Console and the Board are one thing, with one name: The Council.** No split.
- The Council is a governing body *and* an advisory circle at once — government + ring of
  trust. You talk to the Council; it answers back through Phylax.
- The members are **governors** — each governs one domain.
- **"Hetairoi" is dropped as a separate collective label.** The room already has a name
  (the Council); we don't also label the group. Etymology can stay as flavor, not as a name.
- **Zenod is not the boss.** It governs Memory, peer to the rest. The power is the table,
  not any one seat. (This is a deliberate shift away from "Zenod is the important one.")

### Historical anchor

Alexander ran exactly this shape, across three structures the name blends:
- **Satraps** — governors he placed over provinces ("governors of things").
- **The Hetairoi (Companions)** — his inner circle of noble advisors (the ring of trust).
- **The Somatophylakes** — the seven bodyguards, highest honor, closest to his person —
  which is precisely where **Phylax** sits: the guard at the door.

## The governors — one line each

| Governor | Governs | One-liner |
|---|---|---|
| **Zenod** | Memory | Keeps everything you've ever fed it; turns evidence into meaning, never forgets. |
| **Archus** | the Plan | Turns memory into a backlog of runnable work. Charts the course. |
| **Epaminon** | Execution | Runs the approved work, commands the agents, brings back results. |
| **Phylax** | the Door | Guards your attention — and is the voice the Council speaks back to you through. |
| **Callisthenes** | the Word | Drafts and sends everything that leaves — posts, replies, mail — only on your word. |
| **Nectary** | the Treasury | Turns the backlog into funded work. |

## Decisions captured (and what they overrode)

- **Name of the body = The Council.** Alternative considered and parked: **The Board**
  ("your board of governors") — same structure, more corporate register. Council won on feel.
- **Keep Callisthenes for the Word.** An earlier suggestion to swap in "Hermes" (to dodge a
  name clash with the IdeaScraper daily-tweet bot already called Callisthenes) is reversed —
  a tweet bot *is* an outbound-herald function, so the shared name is consistent, not a clash.
- **Phylax is promoted into the roster.** It previously lived only in `docs/PHYLAX-GATEKEEPER.md`,
  not in the suite roster.
- **Full Greek names are flavor, not spoken names.** Per `Naming.md`, there is no "Zeno" /
  "Zenodotus" persona — the librarian is **Zenod**, full stop. Etymology may be glossed.
