Cookies and browser storage

Effective 16 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-16.

Zenod uses browser storage for sign-in, security, and requested preferences. The application does not set advertising cookies. Blocking essential storage can prevent sign-in or interrupt setup.

Essential sign-in cookies

  • zenod_customer_session: keeps your signed-in session, for up to seven days. Signing out clears it.
  • zenod_google_oidc_flow: protects a Google sign-in attempt, for up to ten minutes.
  • zenod_google_drive_vault_flow: protects a Drive connection attempt, for up to ten minutes.

Preferences and setup

Local browser storage remembers your theme and channel setup progress until cleared or updated. Session storage carries a selected plan during signup and normally ends with the browser tab’s session. You can remove these entries through your browser’s site-data controls.

Other services

Google, GitHub, and Stripe operate their own sign-in and checkout pages and may use their own cookies under their policies. This notice covers Zenod’s application storage. We will update this notice and request consent where required before adding non-essential tracking.
