Last updated: DRAFT (2026-07-04). This policy explains what data the hosted Zenod / Council service, operated by Alpha9 ("we", "us"), collects and how we use it.

1. The short version

Your project data — memory, backlog, agent outputs — lives in your own Git vault repository, not in a shared database we own. We hold the minimum needed to run your account: billing details (via Stripe), the credentials you connect, operational logs, and model-usage metering.

2. What we collect

  • Account & billing: email, and payment records held by our processor Stripe (we do not store card numbers).
  • Connected-account credentials: OAuth tokens / app installations for the services you connect (GitHub, X, Google Drive, etc.), stored per-tenant and encrypted, never written into your vault repo.
  • Vault content: the notes, memory, and backlog your Council creates — stored in your repository. We access it only to operate your tenant.
  • Usage metering: per-call model tokens and cost, kept in your tenant's local ledger to power the credit meter and your usage dashboard.
  • Operational logs: limited technical logs for reliability and support.

3. How we use it

To provision and operate your Council, meter and bill usage, provide support, keep the Service secure, and comply with law. We do not sell your data. We do not use your vault content to train models.

4. Sub-processors and model providers

Model calls are routed to LLM providers (e.g. via OpenRouter/our metering gateway) to generate agent responses. Payments run through Stripe. Hosting runs on our infrastructure providers (e.g. Hetzner) via Dokploy. Each processes data only to deliver its part of the Service. A current sub-processor list is available on request and will be maintained here before launch.

5. Where data lives

Your tenant runs in an isolated single-tenant container; your vault repository lives on GitHub under the account you designate. Hosting region is documented at signup.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and billing records as required for tax and accounting. On deletion we export your vault repository to you and destroy your container volume and stored credentials. Usage ledgers are destroyed with the tenant volume.

7. Your rights

You can access, export, correct, or delete your data. Because your vault is a Git repository you control, most of your content is already directly portable. For everything else, contact us.
