Privacy Policy

Effective 29 August 2026 · Version 2026-08-29. This policy explains how Alpha Analytics SL, trading as Zenod, processes personal data as the controller for hosted Zenod. Our business address is Aribau 168, 1º 1ª, 08036 Barcelona, Spain. Contact: jordi@alpha9.io.

1. The short version

Your knowledge vault lives in a GitHub repository or app-created Google Drive folder you control. Hosted Zenod also processes the minimum account, tenant, credential, usage, and operational data needed to provide and secure the Service.

2. What we collect

  • Account & billing: email, and payment records held by our processor Stripe (we do not store card numbers).
  • Connected-account credentials: scoped OAuth tokens or app installations for services you connect, stored in encrypted tenant-scoped custody and never written into the vault.
  • Vault content: the notes, memory, transcripts, and extracted records your Zenod instance creates - stored in your selected GitHub repository or Drive vault. We access it only to operate your tenant.
  • Usage metering: per-call model, token, operation, and cost records used for limits, reliability, and the account dashboard.
  • Operational logs: limited technical logs for reliability and support.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process account, tenant, vault, and usage data as necessary to perform the service contract; security and limited operational logs for our legitimate interests in preventing abuse and keeping the Service reliable; billing and tax records to comply with legal obligations; and optional integrations or marketing only with consent where consent is required. We do not sell personal data or use vault content to train our models.

4. Sub-processors and model providers

Google and GitHub provide optional identity and customer-owned storage access; Stripe processes checkout, subscriptions, tax data, and invoices; Hetzner hosts the service; and configured model providers, including providers reached through OpenRouter, process prompts needed to generate responses. Providers may process data outside the EEA under their published transfer safeguards. Ask us for the current sub-processor list.

5. Where data lives

The hosted service runs in the EU. Customer data is logically isolated by tenant inside the shared runtime and tenant-prefixed storage. Your vault lives in the GitHub account or Google Drive account you designate. Drive OAuth tokens are kept in encrypted tenant-scoped custody, not in the Drive vault itself. Rebuildable local cache and transaction journals may temporarily mirror vault data on Zenod infrastructure.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep active account data while the Service is provided. Following a verified deletion request, we revoke credentials and remove tenant runtime data from active systems within 30 days, subject to backup rotation and legal holds. Billing and tax records are kept for legally required periods. Routine operational logs are normally kept for up to 30 days unless needed to investigate an incident.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and may withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. Because your vault consists of ordinary Markdown in storage you control, most content is already portable. Email us to exercise a right. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local supervisory authority.

8. Security, cookies, and children

We use encryption in transit, encrypted credential custody, tenant-scoped authorization, signed sessions, restricted tokens, and tested backups. No system is perfectly secure; report concerns to the contact below. Zenod uses essential sign-in and security cookies and does not use advertising cookies. The hosted Service is not directed to children under 18.

9. Contact and changes

Privacy requests: jordi@alpha9.io. We will post material policy changes here and update the effective date.
