Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-16. This policy explains how Alpha Analytics SL, trading as Zenod, processes personal data as the controller for hosted Zenod. Our business address is Aribau 168, 1º 1ª, 08036 Barcelona, Spain. Contact: support@zenod.dev.

1. The short version

Your knowledge vault lives in a GitHub repository or app-created Google Drive folder you control. Hosted Zenod also processes the minimum account, tenant, credential, usage, and operational data needed to provide and secure the Service.

2. What we collect

  • Account & billing: provider account identifier, name, email, profile image when supplied, subscription status, and payment records held by our processor Stripe (we do not store card numbers).
  • Connected-account credentials: scoped OAuth tokens or app installations for services you connect, stored in encrypted tenant-scoped custody and never written into the vault.
  • Vault content: the notes, memory, transcripts, and extracted records your Zenod instance creates - stored in your selected GitHub repository or Drive vault. We access it only to operate your tenant.
  • Usage metering: per-call model, token, operation, and cost records used for limits, reliability, and the account dashboard.
  • Operational logs: limited technical logs for reliability and support.

Google sign-in and Google Drive access

Google sign-in provides your basic profile and verified email to create and secure your account. It does not give Zenod access to your whole Drive. If you choose a Drive vault, a separate permission request uses drive.file for files created by or specifically shared with Zenod. We use that access to read and write your chosen vault and maintain its history and recovery records.

Zenod’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used to provide the features you request, not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Relevant content may be sent to service providers to perform requested processing, subject to those restrictions.

You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops dependent features but does not cancel billing or delete existing vault files. For account deletion, contact support using the process below.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process account, tenant, vault, and usage data as necessary to perform the service contract; security and limited operational logs for our legitimate interests in preventing abuse and keeping the Service reliable; billing and tax records to comply with legal obligations; and optional integrations or marketing only with consent where consent is required. We do not sell personal data or use vault content to train our models.

4. Sub-processors and model providers

Google and GitHub provide optional identity and customer-owned storage access; Stripe processes checkout, subscriptions, tax data, and invoices; Hetzner hosts the service; and configured model providers, including providers reached through OpenRouter, process prompts needed to generate responses. Providers may process data outside the EEA under their published transfer safeguards. See our service-provider categories. You can request the current providers and applicable transfer safeguards from support. Optional WhatsApp and Telegram channels also involve their respective messaging providers. Support email is handled through Google Workspace.

5. Where data lives

The hosted service runs in the EU. Customer data is logically isolated by tenant inside the shared runtime and tenant-prefixed storage. Your vault lives in the GitHub account or Google Drive account you designate. Drive OAuth tokens are kept in encrypted tenant-scoped custody, not in the Drive vault itself. Rebuildable local cache may temporarily mirror vault data on Zenod infrastructure. Transaction journals are durable recovery records while the tenant is active; Drive-side journals and reversible tombstones live under the vault's `.zenod/` control folder and are retained indefinitely under the current policy.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep active account data while the Service is provided. Following a verified deletion request, we revoke credentials and remove tenant runtime data from active systems within 30 days, subject to backup rotation and legal holds. Billing and tax records are kept for legally required periods. Routine operational logs are normally kept for up to 30 days unless needed to investigate an incident.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and may withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. Because your vault consists of ordinary Markdown in storage you control, most content is already portable. Email us to exercise a right. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity before acting. We normally respond within one month; if an extension is permitted and needed, we explain it within that period. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local supervisory authority. We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. AI-generated memory and answers are product outputs for you to review.

8. Security, cookies, and children

We use encryption in transit, encrypted credential custody, tenant-scoped authorization, signed sessions, restricted tokens, and tested backups. No system is perfectly secure; report concerns to the contact below. Zenod uses essential sign-in and security cookies and does not use advertising cookies. See the Cookies and browser storage notice for purposes and durations. The hosted Service is not directed to children under 18.

Providing data and business use

Account and billing information is needed to provide a paid account; without it we cannot provide those features. Storage and messaging connections are optional, but the corresponding features will not work without access. Do not submit personal data you are not entitled to share.

This policy describes our account and service processing. If your organization needs Zenod to process personal data on its behalf, contact support to arrange the required data-processing terms before submitting that data. This Privacy Policy is not a substitute for a data-processing agreement.

9. Contact and changes

Privacy requests: support@zenod.dev. We will post material policy changes here and update the effective date.
