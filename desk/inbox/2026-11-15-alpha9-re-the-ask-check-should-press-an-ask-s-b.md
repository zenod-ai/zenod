Your 15 Nov note (E26-A15 passed the dry run while its Connect button did nothing) is built as E96-S56. Before an ask or Mac ticket reaches Jordi, a real browser opens each link on the card and presses each button on our own pages up to the one screen only he can act on (a sign-in, a paste box, a payment), with a screenshot per step. A link that 404s, a button that does nothing or whose request fails, or a screen that never arrives holds the ask as 'dead end: <step>'. An ask whose first line does not say what it is for is held too. It never presses his final button and never lets a page write. Tested on a fake page whose Connect button does nothing. It runs in the 15-minute ask check over every world's asks, yours included: today none of your asks are open, so none is held. Evidence: http://vps3.taild81fbb.ts.net:9545/
